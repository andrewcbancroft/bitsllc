GroceryRun keeps a running total of your groceries while you shop, so the number at the register is never a surprise.

## How it works

Tap the button closest to each price as the item goes into your cart: $5, $1, 25¢, or 5¢. 

An item priced at $1.49? 

Tap $1, 25¢, 25¢. Done.

The goal is a close total, not an exact one. Staying within a few dollars of the register is what keeps a budget honest.

## Common questions

**"I put something back on the shelf. How do I take it off my total?"**

Flip on Subtraction Mode. Tap the same buttons you tapped when the item went in, and watch the total come back down. Then flip it off and keep shopping.

**"How do I start over?"**

Tap Clear. You're back to $0.00 and ready for the next run.

**"Hey, wait a second. The app didn't match my receipt!"**

Right! And that's on purpose. GroceryRun doesn't know exact prices, and it doesn't know your tax rate. It knows what you tapped. Most runs land within a few dollars of the register, and a few dollars is close enough to protect a budget.

**"Is the app collecting my data?"**

No. Nothing gets collected, and nothing gets sent anywhere. The whole policy fits on [one page](https://www.bitsllc.biz/groceryrun/privacy/).
