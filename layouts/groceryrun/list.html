What you get

  • Four big buttons that cover any price in a few taps
  • A running total of your grocery bill in big, clear type
  • Subtraction Mode for put-backs and changed minds
  • Clear, for starting your next run at $0.00
  • Full VoiceOver support if you use a screen reader

What you don't get

  • No account, no setup
  • No ads, no tracking, no data collection
  • No typing prices with a decimal point while your cart drifts into the cereal display

I built GroceryRun because I needed it. My budget was often tight, and I needed a way to curb my grocery spending before I got to the register. GroceryRun solved that problem by keeping me on track as I shop. I hope it can do the same for you.
