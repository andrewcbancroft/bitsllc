# GroceryRun Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 3, 2026**

GroceryRun keeps a running total of your estimated grocery bill while you shop so you'll never be surprised at the register.

## What GroceryRun collects

Nothing.

Not your name. Not your location. Not your shopping habits. 

Not even "anonymized usage data."

The app never connects to the internet.

There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no third-party anything inside it.

## What stays on your device

The running total you build while shopping lives in the app on your phone, and it goes
away when you tap Clear.

If a future version adds settings (a saved tax rate, for example), those will live in the app's private storage on your device too.

None of this information will ever leave your device.

## Children's privacy

GroceryRun collects nothing from anyone, and "anyone" includes children.

## If this policy ever changes

If some future version of GroceryRun ever collects anything (say, an optional feature
that syncs data you choose to share), I'll update this policy before that version ships
and call the change out in the release notes.

You won't find out by surprise.

Surprises are the thing this app seeks to avoid.
